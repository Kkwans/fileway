Coil3 loading stays on the app-owned image/thumbnail loaders and authenticated loopback capabilities. Persistent caching retains existing quotas. With caching disabled or unavailable, a bounded temporary working file is deleted on cancellation or leaving the page; it is not added to the persistent image cache.
